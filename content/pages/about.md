> Software is less about software and more about people.

Hello and welcome to the Internet home of Ajinkya Bawaskar!

The extent to which technology has influenced our day-to-day life amazes me. I feel that software, in particular has changed the course of human history, in many many incredible ways. I aspire to write software that solves real-world problems and impact the society as whole.

I am a Senior Associate / Software Engineer 3 at JPMorganChase in Mumbai, where I design and develop key features for the Corporate & Investment Bank's Derivatives & FX Settlement platform — scaling it from 100K to over 1M trades processed daily, settling upward of $3T in transaction value daily. The platform is built on Spring Boot microservices communicating over Apache Kafka, with Oracle 19c for storage and Apache Gemfire for caching, deployed to Kubernetes via Jenkins.

I am the de facto escalation point for a 40-person team across India and NA on urgent production issues, and work day-to-day on sync-vs-async design over Kafka, caching strategies, idempotency/retry design, and topic/data partitioning. I also build GenAI agents in production: an AI-powered on-call support agent (FastMCP) that cuts 30-40 minute manual triage down to under a minute, and a Jenkins CI/CD agent on the CIB Marketplace adopted by 1,000+ engineers.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer at GlobalLogic (Dec 2020 - Apr 2022) working on new product development for Verizon 5G routers, and a Software Development Intern at MeNew (Mar 2019 - Aug 2019) working on a restaurant management POS app. I hold a Bachelor of Engineering in Computer Science from Amravati University (2016 - 2020) with a GPA of 9.24/10.

I am writing this blog both, as a record of what my thoughts are at a point of time and also to help anyone who might be on the same journey as me!
